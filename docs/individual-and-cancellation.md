# Subscription tiers and lunch cancellation
Business: $48/month or $480/year. Individual: $29/month or $290/year. Applicable taxes are calculated in Stripe. Price IDs and catalog version live in src/lib/domain/paid-access.ts.
All original 14-day trials retain Business access. Adding a card does not reset or shorten the trial. A verified setup schedules the selected plan for the original trial end. No existing live paid subscriptions existed at rollout. Legacy Business prices remain recognized for entitlement reconciliation but are not offered for new checkout.
Individual includes maps, accounts, road routing, check-ins, visits, notes, tasks and calendar. Lunch & Learns, meeting requests, reports, spending and team administration require Business. UI upgrade locks are backed by server and public-link checks. Live Stripe approval determines paid entitlements; local plan/status alone cannot grant access.
The live Stripe customer portal exposes only the four new prices. Trial plan changes continue the trial; upgrades use Stripe-reviewed prorations and decreasing price / shorter interval changes are scheduled at period end.
Owners or team managers can cancel pending invitations or upcoming lunches. Cancellation saves the reason, revokes booking links, stops pending reminders and removes internal calendar mapping and spending commitments while preserving recorded spending and history. Existing external catering orders require acknowledgement and must be canceled with the vendor separately.
Office notification is optional and defaults on. The office email includes the reason and rep contact. Delivery has a durable idempotent outbox and retry action; cancellation is not reversed by delivery failure. A calendar cancellation attachment reuses the booking UID; external calendar behavior is controlled by the recipient application.
Cancellation and approval/confirmation races use row locks. Completed lunches are preserved. Verification uses a disposable PostgreSQL database, example.invalid recipients and simulated browser routing/Stripe responses; it does not mutate production lunches or send office emails.
