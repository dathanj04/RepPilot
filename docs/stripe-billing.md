# Subscription selection and original-trial card setup

Subscriptions always displays the existing approved Business monthly ($58.65) and Business annual ($591.60) options, including in the native shell. Both are Business billing intervals, not different feature tiers. No Individual price or unimplemented tier is invented.

The original server-created 14-day trial is authoritative and is never restarted by selecting a plan, adding a card, logging in, or canceling a subscription. Saving a preference alone authorizes no payment.

Optional card setup requires explicit consent to the recurring price, applicable tax, first-charge date and cancellation terms. During a live trial Stripe Checkout runs in setup mode, with no subscription charge or automatic_tax parameter. After verified completion, the app creates a Stripe subscription with the selected approved price, verified SetupIntent payment method and the original trial_end. Direct Subscription creation handles the final 48 hours that subscription-mode Checkout cannot preserve. Late confirmation after expiry uses trial_end=now rather than granting another trial. Stripe handles automatic collection when the trial ends; failed or authentication-required payments never grant paid access.

Checkout, completion and recovery share a per-workspace PostgreSQL advisory transaction lock. Only the latest database-bound completed Setup Checkout is eligible; customer, organization, plan, livemode, consent and SetupIntent ownership/status are verified against Stripe. Stripe subscription creation is idempotent by completed Setup Checkout ID. A permanent database audit receipt and Stripe setupCheckoutId metadata prevent canceled subscriptions from being recreated when old completion events are replayed after Stripe’s idempotency-key retention expires. Existing nonterminal subscriptions prevent duplicates. Managed Payments is explicitly disabled on Checkout to preserve the existing merchant-owned Stripe Tax flow and permit setup mode. Matching open Checkouts resume; a different open choice expires before replacement.

Signed webhooks are required. The existing /api/webhooks/stripe endpoint reconciles checkout.session.completed setup events and current subscription/invoice state. Subscriptions also reconciles the server-bound session against Stripe as recovery for delayed delivery; query strings never prove payment. Every protected request still verifies live paid entitlement after the original trial. Expired unpaid users are directed to Subscriptions, while support and deletion remain available.

Native card and portal actions navigate to Stripe's HTTPS external origin, which Capacitor opens in the system browser. No card data is collected or stored inside RepPilot. The external-purchase UI is intended for the U.S. iOS storefront. International storefronts and a Google Play release need their applicable store purchase-channel configuration reviewed before distribution.

Production requires STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET. Keep the key restricted where practical: customer read/write, Checkout session read/write (including expire), SetupIntent read, subscription read/write, invoice read and portal-session write. Never commit credentials. Existing tax-exclusive prices and Stripe Tax registration are preserved. Live Tax Settings and Texas registration were re-read; a noncharging invoice preview returned automatic_tax complete with taxability_reason=taxable_basis_reduced. Stripe Tax only collects where an active registration applies. No registration, account tax settings or customer subscription was modified for that check.

Automated validation covers exact trial boundaries (including one hour left), delayed confirmation, no second trial, no early subscription payment, explicit consent, approved prices, payer ownership, verified SetupIntent ownership, replay and duplicate protection, matching-session reuse, mismatched-session expiry, signed webhooks and fail-closed entitlements. Test payment flows must use the separate RepPilot AI sandbox; Preview shares the live database and must not be used for simulated purchases.
